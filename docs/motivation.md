# Motivation

> Open this every morning. It takes 2 minutes.

## Why this matters
Two freshers are building a real platform for a real factory: the website importers will see, the tool employees will use every day, and the board that tracks real orders. Most people get their first chance like this years into their career. You got it now. That's not pressure. It's a head start.

## The daily 2-minute ritual
1. Read yesterday's line in the **wins log** below.
2. Look at `track.md` and pick **one** checkbox you'll close today. Just one. More is a bonus.
3. Write it in one sentence: *"Today I will ___."*
4. At the end of the day, add one line to the wins log, even if the win is "I finally understood X".

## Things to remember on hard days
- **Stuck for 30 minutes?** Stop. Write down what you expected and what happened, then ask Claude, your teammate or your senior friend. Being stuck for 3 hours isn't brave, it's expensive.
- **Not knowing is the job.** Seniors don't know everything. They know how to find out quickly. That's the skill you're building.
- **Slow is smooth, smooth is fast.** A tested small step beats a big untested leap every time.
- **"It works on my machine" is not done.** Done means tested, reviewed, demoed.
- **Behind schedule isn't failure. Hiding it is.** Say it early, re-plan, move on.
- **Compare yourself with yesterday's you,** not with someone on LinkedIn.
- **Rest is part of the plan.** Tired code is buggy code. Sunday is off for the platform, and for you too.

## A line for each day of the week
- **Mon:** Start small. Ship something by lunch.
- **Tue:** Read one piece of code you didn't write, and understand it.
- **Wed:** Write the test first, today, just once.
- **Thu:** Explain what you built to your teammate in 2 minutes.
- **Fri:** Demo it. Visible progress builds trust.
- **Sat:** Catch up, learn one concept deeply, update the docs.
- **Sun:** Off. Really.
