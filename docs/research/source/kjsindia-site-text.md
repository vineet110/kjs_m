Processing (Blending & Ball Milling)
Home
Processing
Blending
Ingredient blending is one of the most critical steps in producing high-quality food products. As a contract manufacturing partner, KJS ensures precision, consistency, and safety across every batch. Many leading brands rely on our expertise for dependable dry powder blending and customized formulations.
Dry Powder Blending
KJS delivers high-quality dry blending with strict process control and attention to detail. Our systems are designed to handle a wide range of ingredients while maintaining uniformity and product integrity.
High-Quality Food Blending Services
Our blending operations meet the quality standards expected from top-tier custom food manufacturers. Every blend undergoes controlled processing, ensuring homogeneity, stability, and scalability.
OUR CAPABILITIES
Ribbon Blending
KJS operates six full-scale industrial ribbon blenders engineered for efficient, uniform mixing. The inner ribbon moves material outward, while the outer ribbon pushes it inward toward the central discharge, creating a counter-directional flow for superior homogeneity.
The gentle, fluid mixing motion allows for blending times of 10–20 minutes, ideal for powders, premixes, nutraceutical blends, seasoning mixes, and more.
Confectionery Manufacturing – Ball Milling
In addition to dry blending, KJS offers ball milling for confectionery applications, enabling precise particle-size reduction, smooth texture development, and consistent mass preparation for chocolates, cocoa-based products, and specialty confectionery bases.

Food warehousing is a crucial link between food processing and distribution to the consumer. Regulations alone require a incredible level of detail in how we maintain and control both product flow and conditions in our warehouses. Here are just a few issues KJS monitors very closely when it comes to your products:
Pest control. From ants and insects to rodents and birds, everything wants to eat your food products. We are on guard to prevent all pest access to your products and diligently monitor the premises Sanitation. As a result of Coronavirus, we have set our standards in line with govt. requirements, but also go above and beyond. We measure temperatures of every employee every day. We send home anyone with a fever. Plant access is given only to those vital to operations. We sanitize common spaces and surfaces multiple times a day. We take sanitisation very seriously and that starts with our people, Personnel.

QUALITY CONTRACT FOOD MANUFACTURING
QUALITY CONTRACT FOOD MANUFACTURING
Quality is at the core of our culture. We demand our facility always be audit ready, prepared even if you decide to audit us unannounced. We proudly ace plant audits by the most stringent food auditors from some of the industry’s best.
COMPONENTS OF OUR APPROACH
Our quality control in food production processes hinge on being proactive with a comprehensive set of policies and procedures.
Fully functioning HACCP plans that include screens, magnets, and metal detection
A diligent monitoring infrastructure to ensure allergen control and prevent micro contamination
Dust collection at multiple points in every production line, especially source points
Master processing room sanitation schedules as well as cleaning procedures designed specifically for each piece of equipment
Preventative maintenance schedules and procedures designed specifically for each piece of equipment
Self-improvement programs for employees
Rigorous visitor and subcontractor security protocols
INTERNAL TESTING
Sieve Testing – particle size distribution
Bulk Density – measurement of compactness
Moisture – moisture level of products
pH – acidity or alkalinity of products
EXTERNAL TESTING
Micro Testing – pathogenic, nonpathogenic and spoilage microorganism levels
Precision Means Quality
